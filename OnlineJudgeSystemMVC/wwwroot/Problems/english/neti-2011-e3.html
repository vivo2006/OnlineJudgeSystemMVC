Gift

National Autumn Informatics Tournament, Shumen, 25-27 November 2011. Group E (grades 4-5). Original problem E3. Author: Kinka Kirilova-Lupanova.

For CodeXpert, read from standard input and write to standard output. Use the time and memory limits displayed on the problem page.

For her first day of school, Svetla received a backpack with a combination lock. Its password contains six characters: three distinct uppercase Latin letters and three distinct digits.

Positions 1, 3, and 5 contain letters, ordered alphabetically. Positions 2, 4, and 6 contain digits, ordered from largest to smallest.

Svetla remembers the letters and digits but not their order. Write a program gift that reconstructs the password so she can open her backpack.

Input

The first line contains three distinct uppercase Latin letters followed by three distinct digits, as a sequence of six characters.

Output

Print the six-character password.

Sample

Input

WKN387

Output

K8N7W3
